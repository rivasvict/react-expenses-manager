# Category trends: polish

Three follow-ups to the shipped [category trends](../category-trends/feature-brief.md).

1. **Empty list.** When no category has entries in the range, the card offers
   "Add expense" / "Add income". Trends is a place to read, not to record, so the
   card must not offer to add anything.
2. **Back link.** On one category's trend, the gold "‹ All categories" link sits
   on its own row between the Savings | Categories switch and the month header.
   It feels disjointed: another row, another colour, far from the thing it
   leaves.
3. **Per-month colours.** Per month draws a gold line. For an expense category a
   rise should read as bad (red) and a fall as good (green); for an income
   category the reverse.

Out of scope: the Change view (already red/green against the reference month),
the ranked list rows, the picker sheet.
