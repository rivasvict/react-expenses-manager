Expenses Tracker Design System

A calm, dark fintech system built on a single gold accent.

A deep, low-contrast neutral scale carries the interface; a warm gold accent marks the brand and the one primary action per screen. Semantic green and rose separate money in from money out. Every value on this page is drawn directly from the app's design tokens.
